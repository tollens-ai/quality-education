# Bach and Bolton on ritual testing

Researched 2026-09-24. **Check every quote word for word against the live page before it goes on
screen.** The research tool returned extracts, not raw text.

## Claims that support the series

| Claim | Quote | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Checking without purpose is ritual | "just a demonstration done perhaps for the entertainment of others or for ritualistic/magical purposes, such as to appease management" | Bach & Bolton, [Testing and Checking Refined](https://www.satisfice.com/blog/archives/856) (2013, revised) |
| Checking vs testing | "Testing encompasses checking… whereas checking cannot encompass testing." | same |
| Going through the motions | "the justification for the formality is based on rumours and on going through the motions" | Bolton, [Sock Puppets of Formal Testing](https://developsense.com/blog/2014/07/sock-puppets-of-formal-testing) (2014) |
| Pathetic compliance (Bach's term) | "following 'the rules' so we don't 'get in trouble'—a pre-school level of motivated behaviour" | Bolton's gloss, same post |
| Test cases aren't testing | "Tests cannot be encoded. What can be encoded are notes about testing." | Bach & Hodder, [Test Cases Are Not Testing](https://www.satisfice.com/download/test-cases-are-not-testing) |
| A passing test is weak evidence | "A passing test is an observation that the program appeared to work correctly, under some set of conditions that we were conscious of (and many that we weren't)." | Bolton, [Why Pass vs. Fail Rates Are Unethical](https://developsense.com/blog/2012/02/why-pass-vs-fail-rates-are-unethical) (2012) |
| Testing isn't QA | "Testing does not assure the quality of the product. Testing helps to assure awareness of the quality of the product." | Bolton, [Quality Assurance and Testing](https://developsense.com/blog/2024/01/quality-assurance-and-testing) (2024) |
| LLMs repeat folklore | "ChatGPT is not designed to produce answers that are right. It's designed to produce answers that sound good." | Bolton, [Response to Testing Bolt-On AI](https://developsense.com/blog/2023/08/response-to-testing-bolt-on-ai) (2023) |
| Operators are responsible | "The operator of an AI agent always bears responsibility for the behavior of that agent." | Bach, Bach & Bolton, [Responsibility is the Human Moat](https://www.satisfice.com/blog/archives/488082) (2026) |

## Terms to credit
- **Testing vs checking**: Bach & Bolton (2009; refined 2013 onwards).
- **Pathetic compliance**: James Bach. The primary source hasn't been found; Bolton's gloss is cited above.
- **Factory School**: Bret Pettichord, "Four Schools of Software Testing" (2003).
- **Cargo cult software engineering**: Steve McConnell, *IEEE Software* 17(2), 2000. It builds on
  Feynman's "Cargo Cult Science" (1974): "the planes don't land". Nobody in the Rapid Software
  Testing community uses "cargo-cult testing" as a term. **Sensitivity:** the "cargo cult" framing
  is criticised as demeaning to Melanesian peoples. Don't depict it visually.

## Where their framing differs from Ed Pringle's
- They broadly agree that testing includes checking.
- Bach and Bolton add that checking can happen with no testing around it, and then it is ritual.
  That is exactly the failure Qing describes.
- They separate testing sharply from quality assurance. The series shouldn't treat "testing" and
  "doing quality" as the same thing.
