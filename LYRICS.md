# Lyric craft

The entry point for writing and reviewing lyrics for *Software Quality Theory 101*.
Qing is the domain expert and the songwriting ear. Her dated corrections are preserved in the
focused references below and in each episode’s expert notes.

Use the [songwriting skill](.claude/skills/songwriting/SKILL.md) for the active workflow; this
guide states the priorities that govern drafting and review tradeoffs. (The abandoned
code-composition workflow is [archived](archive/code-composition/README.md).)

## Meaning first, every axis checked

Meaning governs drafting tradeoffs, and all six axes (meaning, tone/connotation, place in the
story, scansion, stress and rhyme) govern review before anyone hears a draft. Read
[meaning, voice and teaching](docs/lyrics/meaning.md#every-line-every-axis) for literal and
implied claims, series-wide distinctions, natural phrasing, novice clarity, mishearings and
attributed voices.

## Structure and form

Decide whole-song shape and the hook before polishing lines. Read
[structure and examples](docs/lyrics/structure-and-process.md#structure) for keeping the form
lean, choosing examples by what they prove, verse arcs, chorus payoffs and genre-specific form.
Episode 1’s pop form is one worked example, not a checklist of sections to stack into every song.

## Rhythm, rhyme and checking

Lock the beat grid first so stresses, pickups and internal rhyme slots line up cleanly. Read
[rhythm, rhyme and patter](docs/lyrics/rhythm-and-rhyme.md#rhythm) for grids, rhyme families,
General American accuracy, predictable internal slots,
[patter songs](docs/lyrics/rhythm-and-rhyme.md#patter-songs) (and the optional
[parallel-drafting method](.claude/skills/songwriting/references/drafting-with-parallel-drafters.md)
for dense patter lists), and [checking](docs/lyrics/rhythm-and-rhyme.md#checking) for
`music/check/rhyme.py` and dictionary caveats.

## Writing for the performer

Read [performer guidance](docs/lyrics/performers.md#writing-for-the-performer) for phrasing,
active Suno defaults and punctuation, plus the preserved
[MiniMax Episode 1 findings](docs/lyrics/performers.md#generating-the-song-minimax). Check phrase
joins in the actual take.

## Process

Read [revision and scratchbooks](docs/lyrics/structure-and-process.md#process) for scratchbooks,
drafting mode, keeping lyrics ahead of the storyboard and bringing complete options to Qing. New
lessons belong in the reference that owns the decision; see
[maintaining this guide](docs/lyrics/structure-and-process.md#maintaining-the-guide).
