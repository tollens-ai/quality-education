# Episode 1 storyboard: "Who Lives Here?"

One continuous shot, 9:16, 3:20.7, on Qing's chosen take. It follows the approved teaching plan
in [01-you-never-told-me.md](01-you-never-told-me.md): the whole lesson is **quality is value to
people who matter**. Chosen 2026-09-25 from a blind round of nine theme-first concepts (the
funnel is in the episode file). Several ideas here came from the other concepts; they're marked
*(from …)*.

## The world

**A cutaway tower block at night, like a lit dollhouse.** Every flat is a life. Clawd builds in the
basement from a ticket that says only "make it good", its FOR box blank. A glass lift runs up the
middle; the camera rides it. Builders downstairs, lives upstairs, and only the ticket chute
between them.

**Visual grammar** (taught in the first 20 seconds, read all video):
1. **A flat is a person.** Every flat glows warm with its own life (lamplight, a silhouette, one prop).
2. **Gold is value.** A flat turns *gold* only when what's built is good for the person who lives
   there: the glow pours from their device onto them *(the relation, from In Good Hands)*. Warm
   lamplight is just life; gold is served.
3. **Orange is Clawd:** Clawd, its builds, and the **quota tokens** it spends. The quota is a glass
   tube of orange tokens running up the lift shaft wall; every build drains it.
4. **Ink is you.** Your handwriting (dark blue-black on a cream ticket) is the only thing that tells
   Clawd anything. The ticket's printed boxes (FOR · GOOD = · DON'T NEED · COST · FOR YOU) are
   blank until you fill them.
5. **Your flat has the blind down.** Top floor but one, right side. We see your shadow on the blind
   (what is that shape? someone mid-workout, one arm pumping) and your hand at the letterbox. Your
   doorbell glows, never rung. The shadow is always unmistakably exercise, a standing dumbbell
   curl, never a figure hanging from anything.

**Cast.** Clawd, the Claude Code crab (orange, blocky, slit eyes, four stubby legs, arm nubs):
eager, proud, deflated, hopeful. You: a hand with chalk dust and a hair tie, your handwriting, your
shadow. The residents. The bots in the break: Molty (OpenClaw's official lobster), Jolly (Muse's
mascot, drawn faithfully), a Hermes ☤ bot; OpenAI (the exact Blossom mark, with Astra ★, Sol ☀,
Luna ☾) and Grok (its exact mark) appear only as name plates on the directory board; Instinct as a
text entry.

**Look.** Deep night blue and warm lamplight, luminous gold, Clawd orange. Bold dark outlines so
everything reads at phone size; soft light bloom; subtle paper grain; tiny lives everywhere (a cat
stretching, steam from a pan, a plant swaying) that reward a second watch. Fonts: Bricolage
Grotesque for lyrics, Caveat for your handwriting, Pixelify Sans for Clawd's labels.

**Open questions that pull viewers through:** What's the shape on the blind (what are they doing)? Will Clawd ring the
bell? What goes in the FOR box? Each is planted early and paid off late.

## Lyric type

- **Huge** (140–220 px, heavy): the intro, both halves of the hook in every chorus, the line to
  remember in all three pre-choruses, and the break's definition (the biggest: one word per hit).
- **Medium** (64–84 px) in a lower band with a soft scrim, above X's UI zone: verse lines, "or"
  lines, the bridge.
- Never over the main read; the shot leaves room for the words.
- Corner marks: "@yanqingcheng" top left, "∴ tollens" top right, tiny.

## Guardrails (from the teaching plan)

- "or… or…" shows trade-offs with a price, never a pick-one switch.
- The vault door mocks security that doesn't fit this person, and it fits the family.
- "Make it pass" is a student handing it in.
- "No data leak" shows the people whose data it is.
- "Steals the show" is right for the demo: wow is someone's good.
- The agent keeps asking after the brief ("kg or lb?").
- People outside the brief stay lit: they're for other apps.
- The credit is a plain card and ends at 134.7 s, before "I'm someone too".
- Corporate marks appear exactly, on plates; never redrawn as characters.
