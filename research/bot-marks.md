## What this means for the design

- **Corporate marks (OpenAI, Grok, Muse) appear as themselves, reproduced exactly,** on a badge,
  sign or screen next to a character. They are never redrawn as a character's head. Drawing a
  logo in the video's style is itself an alteration.
- **Open-source marks are far safer** to draw as characters: OpenClaw's lobster, Hermes' ☤.
- **Classic symbols** are always available: a star, sun and moon for Astra, Sol and Luna; a lyre
  for Muse; winged sandals for Hermes.
- **The post text says** the video isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any company shown.
- **Check with Qing** that Instinct and Muse mean Spear Street's agent and Meta's agent.
