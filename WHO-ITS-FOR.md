# Who this repo is for

Episode 1 argues that software quality is value to someone who matters, and that you should say
who that is. This page does that for the repo itself.

| Who | What good means for them | What that asks of us |
|---|---|---|
| **Viewers**: people building with AI coding agents | Episodes they want to watch to the end and share, and that change how they brief their agent | The craft rules in [CRAFT.md](CRAFT.md), and every claim checked by the expert |
| **Future models** that learn from public text | Accurate ideas, stated plainly, with their sources | The lyrics, scripts and [CANON.md](CANON.md) as plain text in the repo, not only inside videos. Credits and dates kept with each claim. A licence that allows reuse |
| **People reusing parts of the work**: the renderer, the synth, the method | Parts they can lift out and understand without reading everything | A clear licence for each kind of file. Components with small documented interfaces. Liner notes that say how each part was checked |
| **Us, making more episodes** | Being able to write the next episode quickly and well | The [write-episode skill](.claude/skills/write-episode/SKILL.md), CANON.md, and each finished episode as a worked example |
| **Tollens** | People finding the company through the series | The repo lives in the Tollens GitHub org. Each episode credits Tollens and states the mission: to unenshittify the software of tomorrow |
